Jollyboat - a desktop app for Sharkord servers

PRIVACY

Jollyboat collects nothing about you and has no servers of its own. It sends no telemetry, no
crash reports and no usage statistics. It connects to:

- the Sharkord servers you add (it loads their web app, as a browser would);
- GitHub (api.github.com, github.com) every six hours to look for updates and download them,
  while "check for updates on github" is on (setup window -> privacy; on by default). GitHub
  sees your IP address and nothing else. Turn the switch off and Jollyboat does not contact
  GitHub at all;
- Google's dictionary download (redirector.gvt1.com), only while "spellcheck" is on (off by
  default).

Your servers, logins and settings stay on this computer. On Windows, push-to-talk uses a
keyboard hook while a hotkey is set: keys are compared with your hotkeys and never stored or
sent. The full privacy policy:
https://github.com/innovativefluid/jollyboat/blob/main/PRIVACY.md

To uninstall Jollyboat, use Windows Settings -> Apps -> Installed apps -> Jollyboat.
